**MarchMallows: Steam Capsule Art**

I created the Steam capsule illustrations, in multiple sizes, for *MarchMallows*, a chaotic co-op and team VS puzzle game from Perfect Execution Games. In the game, you guide a bunch of adorable, clueless creatures called Marchies safely home before they march straight into traps.

![](/images/work/march-mallows/mm_main.JPG)

This was a fun project to work on. I started by asking the team for 3D assets and gameplay footage, then used those references to build the capsule designs. The challenge was balancing two things: showing what makes the game unique, and making the design appealing enough that someone scrolling through Steam wants to click and download it.

The game has just launched its Steam page, and I’m hoping it does well.

You can learn more about the game at [marchmallows.perfectexecutiongames.com](https://marchmallows.perfectexecutiongames.com/).
