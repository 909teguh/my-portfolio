**Master of Chaos: Steam Capsule Art**

This was my second project with Perfect Execution Games, and again I created the Steam capsule illustrations in multiple sizes. This time, the style was 2D cartoon.

*Master of Chaos* is an action game about a cute little wizard who counters, deflects, and builds insane combos until he’s strong enough to beat the boss, or break the game entirely.

![](/images/work/master-of-chaos/vertical.JPG)

As before, I asked for reference material and built the capsule designs from it. The challenge was the same: represent what makes the game special, while making the design appealing enough to catch someone’s eye on Steam. Here, that meant capturing the contrast between an adorable wizard and the chaos he unleashes.

The game is currently in development, with a free demo available on Steam.

You can learn more about the game at [masterofchaos.perfectexecutiongames.com](https://masterofchaos.perfectexecutiongames.com/).
