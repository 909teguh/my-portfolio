Feud: The Seer's Prophecy\
\
In 2020, I had the chance to work on the illustrations for a board game called *Feud: The Seer’s Prophecy*, published by Mindgate Studios, a German board game studio. It was my first big project, and it unfolded over a long stretch of time.

The game is set in the world of the Vikings. I created the box cover, the play mat, and several card designs. I also made much of the promotional material, including the Kickstarter logo, banners, and campaign images, along with other artwork for publication.

For the play mat, I designed a map of a Viking village divided into several sections, each with its own function in the game. For the cover, I illustrated the main character alongside a bird’s-eye view of the village.

The project was completed in 2021 and launched on Kickstarter. Within a month, *Feud* reached its $10,000 goal and moved into mass production. In 2022, production wrapped up and the game shipped to backers in many countries, including Indonesia, my home country.

**What I learned**

Along the way, I found out that many details I had lumped together with “European” culture, like the shape of helmets, armor, and houses, were actually very specific to the Vikings. It taught me to understand the context behind an illustration more deeply, and not to hesitate to ask people who know it better.

You can learn more about the game at [mindgate-studios.com/feud](https://www.mindgate-studios.com/feud).
