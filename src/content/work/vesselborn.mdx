VESSELBORN is an ongoing project that I started in 2026. So far, I’ve created concept art for the characters and factions of its universe, along with cover art for the website and the books, including the novella *Thazvaari Nights* and the series episode *Energy Wars: Episode One*.

![](/images/work/vesselborn/vb1.png)![](/images/work/vesselborn/vb5.png)

VESSELBORN is a science-fantasy universe set on Geba, a brown dwarf world with six thousand years of history and the people who live through it. Designing its characters and factions meant thinking about who they are and where they come from, not just how they look.

![](/images/work/vesselborn/vb2.png)![](/images/work/vesselborn/vb3.png)

Everything is drawn by hand, with no generative AI involved.

You can explore the universe at [vesselborn.com](https://vesselborn.com/).
