I designed the poster and the cartridge cover illustration for *Today’s a Bad Day*, a Game Boy game by SushiGummy and WhitetailDeer where the goal is to make everyone miserable.

![](/images/work/today-s-a-bad-day/Screenshot%202026-10-31%20213740.png)

The brief was a classic, retro look, in the spirit of 1980s Nintendo cartridge art. It was a fun challenge to bring that nostalgic feel to a game with such a mischievous premise, and to design something that would look right on a physical cartridge label as well as on a poster.

You can find the game at [sushigummy.itch.io/todays-a-bad-day](https://sushigummy.itch.io/todays-a-bad-day).
