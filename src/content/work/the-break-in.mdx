**The Break-In: Cover Illustrations**

I’ve been working with the developer of *The Break-In* since 2020, creating illustrations for the game’s covers. So far, I’ve made four of them.

![](/images/work/the-break-in/Screenshot%202026-10-31%20221202.png)

*The Break-In* is a four-player online co-op burglary game about sneaking into houses, stores, and casino vaults to steal everything that isn’t bolted down. It also supports VR and non-VR crossplay.

![](/images/work/the-break-in/Screenshot%202026-10-31%20221643.png)

The style here is cartoony and playful, which suits the game’s sense of humor. The challenge is to keep the tone light and funny while still showing the heist at the heart of the game.

The game left Early Access and was fully released in May 2025. You can find it on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1397390/The_BreakIn/).
